# Go Mission - Install Guide Audio Script

## 🎙️ For Audio Recording & Animation

---

# 🇺🇸 ENGLISH VERSION

## INTRO (5 seconds)
**[Warm, friendly tone]**

"Welcome to Go Mission! Let me help you install this app on your phone so you can start your journey with God."

---

## ANDROID INSTRUCTIONS

### Step 1: Open the Menu (8 seconds)
**[Clear, instructional tone]**

"First, look at the top right corner of your screen. You'll see three dots. This is the menu button. Tap on those three dots now."

---

### Step 2: Add to Home Screen (10 seconds)
**[Encouraging tone]**

"Great! Now you'll see a menu appear. Look for the option that says 'Add to Home Screen' or 'Install App.' Scroll down if you don't see it right away. When you find it, tap on it."

---

### Step 3: Confirm Installation (8 seconds)
**[Reassuring tone]**

"A popup will appear asking you to confirm. Just tap 'Install' or 'Add.' This will add Go Mission to your home screen, just like any other app."

---

### Step 4: Open from Home Screen (10 seconds)
**[Excited, celebratory tone]**

"Awesome! Now go to your home screen. You'll see the Go Mission icon there — it's a flame with a cross. Tap on it to open the app. From now on, you can always open Go Mission from here."

---

### Step 5: Enable Notifications (12 seconds)
**[Warm, personal tone]**

"One last thing — when the app asks if you want to receive notifications, please tap 'Allow' or 'Enable.' This way, you'll get updates from your group and daily reminders for your quiet time with God."

---

### CLOSING - ANDROID (5 seconds)
**[Warm, celebratory tone]**

"That's it! You're all set. Welcome to Go Mission. Your journey with God starts today!"

---

## IPHONE INSTRUCTIONS

### Step 1: Tap the Share Button (10 seconds)
**[Clear, instructional tone]**

"First, look at the bottom of your Safari browser. You'll see a square icon with an arrow pointing up — that's the Share button. Tap on it now."

---

### Step 2: Add to Home Screen (12 seconds)
**[Patient, guiding tone]**

"Now you'll see a menu with many options. Scroll down until you see 'Add to Home Screen.' It has a plus sign icon. When you find it, tap on it."

---

### Step 3: Tap Add (8 seconds)
**[Encouraging tone]**

"You'll see a preview of the app. At the top right corner, you'll see the word 'Add.' Tap on it to add Go Mission to your home screen."

---

### Step 4: Open from Home Screen (10 seconds)
**[Excited, celebratory tone]**

"Perfect! Now go to your home screen. You'll find the Go Mission icon there — it's a flame with a cross. Tap on it to open the app. This is now your direct access to Go Mission."

---

### Step 5: Enable Notifications (12 seconds)
**[Warm, personal tone]**

"When the app asks if you want to receive notifications, please tap 'Allow.' This is important so you can get messages from your group and daily reminders for your time with God."

---

### iOS NOTE (8 seconds)
**[Informative tone]**

"By the way, for notifications to work on iPhone, you need iOS 16.4 or newer. If you have an older version, the app will still work, but you won't receive push notifications."

---

### CLOSING - IPHONE (5 seconds)
**[Warm, celebratory tone]**

"That's it! You're all set. Welcome to Go Mission. Your journey with God starts today!"

# 🇵🇭 TAGALOG VERSION

## INTRO (5 seconds)
**[Mainit, magiliw na tono]**

"Maligayang pagdating sa Go Mission! Tutulungan kita i-install ang app na ito sa phone mo para masimulan mo na ang iyong paglalakbay kasama ang Diyos."

---

## ANDROID INSTRUCTIONS

### Step 1: Buksan ang Menu (8 seconds)
**[Malinaw na tono]**

"Una, tingnan mo ang kanang itaas ng iyong screen. May makikita kang tatlong tuldok. Ito ang menu button. Pindutin mo yan."

---

### Step 2: Add to Home Screen (10 seconds)
**[Nakaka-encourage na tono]**

"Maganda! May lalabas na menu. Hanapin mo ang 'Add to Home Screen' o 'Install App.' Mag-scroll ka pababa kung hindi mo agad makita. Pag nakita mo na, pindutin mo."

---

### Step 3: Kumpirmahin ang Installation (8 seconds)
**[Nakaka-assure na tono]**

"May lalabas na popup na magtatanong kung sure ka. Pindutin mo lang ang 'Install' o 'Add.' Ito ay maglalagay ng Go Mission sa home screen mo, parang ibang app."

---

### Step 4: Buksan mula sa Home Screen (10 seconds)
**[Masaya at excited na tono]**

"Ayos! Pumunta ka sa home screen mo. Makikita mo doon ang Go Mission icon — isang apoy na may krus. Pindutin mo para buksan ang app. Mula ngayon, dito mo na palaging mabubuksan ang Go Mission."

---

### Step 5: I-enable ang Notifications (12 seconds)
**[Mainit at personal na tono]**

"Isa pang bagay — kapag nagtanong ang app kung gusto mong makatanggap ng notifications, pindutin mo ang 'Allow' o 'Enable.' Para makatanggap ka ng updates mula sa grupo mo at daily reminders para sa quiet time mo kasama ang Diyos."

---

### CLOSING - ANDROID (5 seconds)
**[Mainit at celebratory na tono]**

"Ayan na! Handa ka na. Maligayang pagdating sa Go Mission. Ang paglalakbay mo kasama ang Diyos ay nagsisimula ngayon!"

---

# 📝 RECORDING TIPS

1. **Voice Style:** Warm, friendly, like a helpful friend — not robotic
2. **Pace:** Slightly slower than normal conversation to allow processing
3. **Pauses:** Add 1-2 second pauses between steps for visual transitions
4. **Emphasis:** Stress action words like "tap," "scroll," "look for"
5. **Tagalog:** Use natural Taglish, not formal Filipino
