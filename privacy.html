Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how Go Mission collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Go Mission website and Android app.

Effective date: June 6, 2026 App package: app.gomission.mobile Website: gomission.netlify.app

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Go Mission, we may collect:

  • Account information such as your name, email address, and profile details.
  • Content you create or share, including messages, group participation, notes, and uploaded images.
  • Device and app information needed to operate the service, such as browser type, device type, app version, and diagnostic data.
  • Push notification tokens so we can deliver app notifications.
  • Audio, microphone, camera, or photo access only when you choose features that require them, such as meetings, camera capture, or media sharing.

How We Use Information

We use information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide discipleship, chat, group, meeting, and communication features.
  • Send important notifications and updates.
  • Improve app stability, security, and user experience.
  • Respond to support requests and maintain the service.

Permissions and Sensitive Features

  • Camera: used only when you choose to capture or share photos or other media.
  • Microphone and audio: used only when you join meetings, record audio, or use voice-related features.
  • Notifications: used to send app alerts, reminders, and communication updates.
  • Photos and uploaded files: used only to support sharing and communication features you initiate.

Third-Party Services

Go Mission may use trusted third-party services to operate the app, including hosting, authentication, cloud storage, analytics, database, and push notification services. These providers may process information on our behalf only as needed to run the service.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With service providers who help us operate Go Mission.
  • When required by law, legal process, or to protect users and the service.
  • Within app features you intentionally use, such as messaging or group participation.

Data Retention and Security

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary to operate the app, maintain records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

  • You can choose whether to grant permissions such as notifications, camera, or microphone.
  • You may stop using the app at any time.
  • You may contact us to ask questions about your information.
