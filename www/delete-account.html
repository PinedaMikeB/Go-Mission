Account Deletion

Request account deletion

Use this page to request deletion of your Go Mission account and the personal data associated with it in the Go Mission app and website.

App: Go Mission Package: app.gomission.mobile Effective date: June 10, 2026
Email deletion request

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to michael.marga@gmail.com from the same email address you used to create your Go Mission account.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Go Mission account.
  3. In the message, include:
    • The email address tied to your Go Mission account
    • Your name on the account (if different from the sender name)
    • A short note if you want your data removed from a specific group (optional)
  4. We will verify that the request comes from the account owner before processing deletion.
  5. After verification, we will delete or deactivate your account and begin removing associated personal data.

You do not need the app installed to submit this request. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account or stored data.

Data that will be deleted

When your deletion request is approved and processed, we delete or anonymize personal data tied to your account, including:

  • Your Go Mission login account (Firebase authentication record)
  • Profile information such as your name, email address, and member profile
  • Journey, training, gospel decision, and progress records linked to your account
  • Group membership links, guest links, and related profile pointers
  • Messages, notes, journal entries, and other content stored under your account
  • Push notification tokens and device notification settings for your account

Data that may be kept

Some information may be retained for a limited time after account deletion:

  • Security and backup copies may be kept in encrypted backups for up to 90 days, then permanently deleted.
  • Support or deletion-request records may be kept for up to 12 months to confirm the request was completed and to prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Legal or compliance records may be kept longer only where required by law.
  • Content you shared inside a group may remain visible to that group in anonymized form (for example, shown as “Deleted user”) where complete removal would affect other members’ records.

Processing time

We aim to complete verified account deletion requests within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once processing is finished.

Questions

For privacy questions or help with this request, contact michael.marga@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
